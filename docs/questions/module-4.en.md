# ADMISSION TEST - MESOCYCLE IV – Defense_Counterattack and Attack

## Question 1

**The defensive zone has three subzones:**

- A) Green, red, and purple
- B) Black, yellow, and blue
- C) Red, yellow, and green
- D) None of the above

**Correct Answer: C**

## Question 2

**Theoretically, the green subzone of the defensive zone conveys:**

- A) Safety, limited shot options, and comfort
- B) Safety, comfort, and control
- C) Safety, pressure from the opponent, and the imminence of losing the point
- D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: B**

## Question 3

**By definition, in padel, defending means:**

- A) Being in the defensive zone
- B) Returning the ball to the other side
- C) Playing an upward-pattern ball
- D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: C**

## Question 4

**The transition zone is located between:**

- A) Sector 1 and 2 of the side mesh
- B) Sector 0 and 1 of the side mesh
- C) Sector 1, 1.5, and the end of sector 1 of the side mesh
- D) None of the above

**Correct Answer: C**

## Question 5

**The transition zone has three subzones:**

- A) Green, yellow, and red
- B) Black, orange, and purple
- C) White, gray, and brown
- D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: A**

## Question 6

**By definition, in padel, attacking means:**

- A) Being in the offensive zone
- B) Defining the point
- C) Executing a downward-pattern shot
- D) Applying maximum intensity to the ball

**Correct Answer: C**

## Question 7

**By definition, the nomenclature given to the zones of the padel court — defensive, transition, and offensive — corresponds respectively to defending, counterattacking, and attacking. This statement is:**

- A) True
- B) False

**Correct Answer: B**

## Question 8

**The offensive zone has three subzones, with the following meaning:**

- A) Red – Area of highest ball pressure index
- B) Yellow – Pressure zone
- C) Green – Management of the position
- D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: D**
