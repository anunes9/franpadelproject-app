# ADMISSION TEST – MESOCYCLE 12 – OFFENSIVE BEHAVIOR IN RESPONSE TO ANGLED LOB (Balão Angulado)

## Question 1

**How many panels make up the mesh structure?**

- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 4
- D) 1

**Correct Answer: B**

## Question 2

**The mesh panels follow a categorization with the top panel being:**

- A) Number 1, the middle 2, and the last 3
- B) Number 3, the middle 2, and the last 1
- C) None of the above
- D) All of the above

**Correct Answer: A**

## Question 3

**The tension along the mesh is distributed in the following order:**

- A) Increasing
- B) Uniform
- C) Decreasing
- D) Parabolic

**Correct Answer: C**

## Question 4

- A) The tension in panel 1 is higher than in panel 3

## Question 5
